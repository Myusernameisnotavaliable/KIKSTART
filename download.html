Add KIKSTART to your Mac Dock

  1. Open KIKSTART in Safari.
  2. Choose File → Add to Dock from the Mac menu bar.
  3. Name it KIKSTART and choose Add.
  4. Launch it from your Dock like an app.

This creates a Safari web app shortcut. It is not the standalone KIKSTART.app installer and requires an internet connection.
